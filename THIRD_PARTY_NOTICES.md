## EFF large word list

`src-tauri/assets/eff_large_wordlist.txt` is the EFF's long word list for random passphrases
(7,776 words), from <https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2016/07/new-wordlists-random-passphrases>,
copyright the Electronic Frontier Foundation and used under the Creative Commons Attribution
license (CC BY) that applies to the EFF's published content. It is used unchanged to
generate passphrases.

## zxcvbn

The passphrase strength estimate uses the `zxcvbn` Rust crate (MIT license), a port of
Dropbox's zxcvbn estimator.
